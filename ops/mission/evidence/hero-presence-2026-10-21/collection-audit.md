## Verdict

All ten actual models render, but this is NOT final photoreal collection approval.
Many occupy noticeably less space than their reference artwork. Current fixed
bounding-sphere framing leaves small details hard to inspect. Enlarging them must
remain safe for the entire revolution, not just one attractive angle. Material,
literal poster identity and natural surface-detail work remain separate art tasks.

## Strengths

Original artwork remains the default visual, opt-in and replay controls are
available, domain identity and financial context remain legible, and the core
four-vital dashboard is intact. User values were not edited; domain selection
changes the active target and its explicit assumptions, not the household cashflow.
No 3D scene replaces the core educational workflow.

## Accessibility Risks And Limits

Icon controls expose names/pressed state in the captured accessibility trees.
Screenshots do not prove reduced motion, keyboard order, physical-device support
or WCAG compliance; standing functional checks remain required. The model canvas
is decorative/aria-hidden, so it must never carry financial information unavailable
elsewhere. An alternative image description is not proof of literal 3D/poster
identity. Small apparent models also make inspection harder at ordinary viewport size.

## Selected Next Investigation

Use actual geometry bounds and a conservative complete-orbit perspective fit to
improve model presence without changing layout, models, maps, lighting or timing.
Do not simply multiply scale, change FOV, shorten the turn, clip narrow screens,
raise caps or weaken visibility/cadence checks. Compare actual rendered desktop/
mobile pixels and all ten fresh HyperFrames exports after any proposed fit.
Current manual screenshots are real wall-clock phases, not frame-exact captures
or all-angle approval. Full-size/front/mid/rest review remains required.
