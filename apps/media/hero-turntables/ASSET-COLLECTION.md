# InterestShield Hero Collection

Updated October 21, 2026. This is a restoration work package, not a production-readiness certificate.

## Contents

- **Full-Resolution-Art**: original generated PNG references for all ten heroes.
- **Web-Posters**: optimized transparent WebP artwork used by the restored dashboard.
- **3D-Studies**: actual self-contained GLB geometry. The refined car is adapted from
  the licensed Car Concept; Vault, House, Land, Credit Card, Student Loan, Medical,
  Personal, Recreation and Custom are original textured candidates.
- **Turntables**: HyperFrames-rendered 1920 x 1080 MP4s, one smooth full revolution
  over eight seconds, followed by a one-second rest.
- **verification.json**: video dimensions, duration, pixel-motion checks and SHA-256 hashes.
- **hero-provenance.json** and **hero-models.json**: source identities and model statistics.

The ten base heroes are Auto, House, Land, Credit Card, Student Loan, Medical,
Personal, Recreation, Custom and Vault. Historical subcategory variants are not
all regenerated in this package.

## Important Art Distinction

The generated artwork and refined models are not interchangeable or exact
reconstructions. The car uses a textured licensed model by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt
Graphics Group GmbH (2024), CC BY 4.0, adapted for InterestShield. Source and license:
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Vault has an open chamber and locking hardware; House has limestone/oak materials
and open glazing; Land has connected trees and a recessed pond; Credit Card has
physical recesses, segmented contacts and a two-sided coin. Student Loan adds
leather spines, cloth, a braided cord, fine tassel and an open spiral diploma.
Medical adds a fitted enamel case, curved guards, chrome hardware and connected
stethoscope. Its updated poster retains the original identity with an emerald
shield; the original edit input and source lineage remain in the repository.
Personal adds a hollow cognac satchel, punched straps, connected brass hardware,
seated physical stitching, an open-bow key and inserted papers, with the original
generated leather source reused explicitly. All posters remain unchanged in this
Personal refinement. Recreation now adds a hollow white/teal yacht, actual cockpit
and window voids, billowed sails, connected rigging and physical panel seams.
Its original oak/cloth sources are explicitly reused; all posters remain unchanged.
Custom adds a recessed brushed-metal compass, seated dial/rose/ticks, faceted
green/red needle, upright hollow lid, actual hinge and bail; all procedural maps
are original and all posters remain unchanged in this slice.
All ten remain candidates, not final photorealism approvals. Material values are
artistic approximations, not measured scans or exact poster reconstructions.

The historical 3D-Studies folder name is retained for compatibility. Its ten models
are now textured candidates with real geometry, portable GLBs and finite turns,
not final art approval. Public reconstruction GPU quota remained unavailable;
authored/licensed refinement did not require a paid job. Collection-wide visual
coherence and fidelity remain explicit production gates. See ASSET-CREDITS.md.

The app defaults to studio artwork. Model inspection is optional. No visual asset
controls financial outputs, and no financial balances are baked into these models.

## Application Status

The restoration corrects minimum-payment budgeting, released payments, retained
calendar cash and selected-target interest scope. Simulator, Vault and both web
and Expo Portfolios use tested funded/equal-liability paths. Native Portfolio has
precise editors, canonical transfer, owner/CAS storage and paired snapshot recovery.
Backend/provider alignment, shared scenario ownership, common-date wealth,
lender/calendar enforcement, physical-device tests and release approval remain
unfinished. Verified previews are draft checkpoints, not production certification.
