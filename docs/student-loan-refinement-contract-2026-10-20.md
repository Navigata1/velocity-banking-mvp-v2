## Material And Delivery Limits

Original generated grayscale cloth/leather images are shader-dyed; approximate
micro-normal and roughness fields and procedural paper grain are authored, not
photographed scans or measured relief/reflectance.512px color/roughness maps are
paired with filtered, renormalized256px normals packed losslessly. Zero codec
angular error is relative to the filtered256px field, not zero loss from512px.

An initial1,435,748-byte build exceeded the original public budget and was rejected
before replacing the old GLB. A lossy normal experiment also failed the unchanged
RMS2/max8-degree guard. No cap or error tolerance was enlarged. The candidate now
used182,272 fewer bytes than the old897,548-byte study. Public total at that checkpoint was
10,232,920 bytes:252,840 bytes below the unchanged10MiB cap. Student Loan also has
a stricter0.75MiB/75,000-unique-triangle limit. The publication lock recalculates
the live collection size before writing, not just the earlier observed total.

## Verification Contract

Nine tests first failed against the old study. Tests decode the distributed GLB
and inspect source hashes, actual texture pixels/PBR, leaf/cover winding/contact,
cap/cord/tassel contact, geometric helix, diploma aperture/spiral and bow holes/
thickness, finite grounded bounds and locked publication dataflow. In-memory
detachment, solid infill, stale-budget and guard-removal probes must be rejected.

Initial7/9 green exposed a test false negative: one-direction vertex proximity
missed genuinely intersecting overhanging faces. Symmetric containment and bounded
segment/triangle/closest-edge checks correct the helper with a smaller tolerance;
crossing-positive and distant-negative probes prevent a permissive helper.
This was not a product-contact failure. Parent independently re-ran9/9 green.

Independent review then found genuine loop self-intersections:90 left and31 right
non-adjacent triangle crossings outside the knot, missed by the original bow test.
A tenth regression observed90 crossings RED; analytic elliptic turns with smaller
ribbon width replace the pinched curves. Decoded surfaces now have no detected
outside-knot crossings or opposing face/normal patches. Crossing-positive and
separated-negative fixtures guard the check. This fixes the confirmed geometry
defect; it is not a claim that every strand/UV is globally watertight/nondegenerate.
Independent re-review cleared the final artifact, reproduced old-loop/face/normal
mutations, and highlighted two coverage gaps. The standing test now additionally
requires closed outward loop surfaces and rejects missing-triangle and jointly
reversed winding/normal mutations. Strand endpoint and ribbon-edge UV limitations
remain explicit; no full-surface watertight or perfect-UV claim is made.
