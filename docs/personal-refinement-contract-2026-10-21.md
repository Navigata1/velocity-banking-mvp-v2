media contracts remained green. Additional fixtures exposed incorrect GPU-instance
expansion, whole-AABB ribbon-width inference and raw normalized-UV inspection.

sections and decoded normalized accessors, retaining true negative fixtures.

Independent review found two P2 defects missed by the first14 cases: papers
crossed the original flap, and real stitches were buried or floating. Actual
exported geometry reproduced both RED. The flap now folds over the front lip,
leaving papers a clear mouth path. Every thread endpoint/center is seated on its
intended surface; the lower flap seam breaks beneath front straps. Rivet contact
and tiny key-bevel self-crossings were also corrected after observed failures.

Front straps use horizontal triangle cuts, an exact small-tolerance weld and
Jacobian normal transport through a smooth bend, preserving the punched outline.
Their cap UVs use useful16bit range; all textured triangles retain nonzero UV area.
A tilted punched fixture disproves smallest-AABB inference: strap-hole rays use
their actualZ punch direction, while still rejecting actual solid infill.

The added contact test checks every real thread instance against actual leather
surfaces at thread scale. Burial uses three-ray interior classification only after
each leather skin proves closed and consistently wound. This is not parity-based
contact on open skins. An exposed acute-edge fixture rejects the ambiguous single-
nearest-normal oracle; a shallow buried-box fixture still fails as intended.
