# House Shadow Study

## Decision

Do not adopt a fixed2048 shadow-map policy across desktop and mobile. The private
study found useful but modest depth, with a substantial increase in submitted
geometry. A separate bounded1024 phone-size comparison is the next experiment.
It is not approved unseen. The current production source and all20 hero
GLB/poster files remain unchanged at verified6ff5568.

## Network Finding Stays Open

The original private capture stopped after baseline desktop art/Replay on ten
unclassified aborted RSC requests. A subsequent collector gathered all four art
cases before enforcing the same failed aggregate network result:4/12/8/6raw
aborts. It still exited1. No request was exempted, filtered away or relabeled.

Metadata-rich baseline diagnostics reproduce prefetch-shaped HTTP200 responses
reported aborted before House/model load. They do not establish intentional
cancellation or a benign cause. A local TLSHTTP1/HTTP2 comparison also observed
aborts in both conditions; HTTP2 was not a sufficient remedy. No app prefetch,
network, timeout, pixel gate, dependency, provider or deployment workaround was
adopted. All diagnostic browsers/servers/owned jobs closed. Temporary diagnostic
certificates were not installed in a trust store or published.

The collector now rechecks raw failures at the aggregate assertion, including a
late callback after an earlier clean snapshot. Six pure cases and four independent
late-arrival controls reject stale passes. This does not rewrite the failed runs.
