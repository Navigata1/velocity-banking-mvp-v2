# Liability Coaching Contract

Classify raw selected-target and LOC balances before normalization: both finite,
nonnegative, with a finite sum. Exactly zero is cleared; nonzero dust is not.
Cleared means no future repayment modeled, not lender payoff confirmation.
Future projections require explicit success, positive safe-integer months and
finite nonnegative interest. Cleared labels are not available payoff projections.

Web and shared Dashboard/Simulator/Cockpit/Vault/Learn preserve this raw cohort.
LOC-only cases model LOC paydown, not a new target chunk. Four dashboard vitals
remain. Cleared interest retains its component breakdown and rounding assumptions.
Known nonpositive cash flow and LOC utilization above (not equal to) 80% remain
warnings independently of invalid or cleared liabilities; unknown inputs do not
establish those warnings. Cleared cash-flow vitals keep their budget risk signal.
Outstanding-cohort warning priorities remain unchanged. Numerical solvers, APR
units, cent tolerance, allocation, normalization and persistence do not change.

Educational monthly model, not financial advice. Browser, device, lender,
backend, photoreal and production approval require separate evidence.
