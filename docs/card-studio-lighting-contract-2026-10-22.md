# Card-Only Shared Studio Lighting

This is a bounded metallic-lighting refinement, not final photoreal approval.
Preserve the calm asset-centered dashboard and existing finite turn/replay.

## Shared Policy

`createHeroEnvironment(renderer, model?)` adds a sixth reflection card ONLY for
the exact `/heroes/creditCard.glb` path. Dimensions are 1.5 x 3, intensity 1.2, center
`[3.73215474204762,-2.468225341834624,3.997359709025046]`; orientation still looks
at `[0,1,0]`. The first five cards, background, 64 x 64 diffusion and PMREM parameters
stay exact. Default, unknown, query-suffixed and other nine model paths use the
original five-card room. Temporary resources dispose on success/PMREM failure.

Web passes its actual model path; HyperFrames passes the validated selected hero
in the same format. Existing asset preparation generates both vendor copies.
No GLB/poster/asset-manifest/material/map/camera/exposure/yaw/clock/math changes.

## Why This Strip

Private fragment measurements cover 1,900 selected records across three surfaces
and four poses, with 11 readouts each, including the actual environment rotation uniform.
The narrower reflector follows the measured rear anisotropic lookup axes while
avoiding the broad panel's measured front axes. Lookup coverage is a geometric
hypothesis, NOT the rough BRDF energy integral. Area 25 to 4.5 with fixed intensity
is not an equal-energy comparison or a universal optimum.

Independent original-size review of all 21 three-way frames approves a bounded
gain: stronger emerald rear at 5 seconds, modest gain at 4 seconds, and a restored
silver gradient at 2 seconds compared with broad fill. The 3/6-second views and
metallic, chip and coin relief remain unfinished. All other
heroes retain their room; no all-ten artwork approval is implied.

Private study checkpoint: 665 artifacts, SHA-256
`52a6748cefa858a613254389de50e9dd4bcd23145f77aec7f10aa06e0017b2da`.
The new private 9-second, 30 fps export has 270 nonblank decoded frames and a stable final
second. The original blank export remains FAILED despite CLI exit 0: its `.mjs` MIME errors
were fixed only in a separate JS/readiness copy. Current exact retained v2 video
has matching manifest and preflight hashes. Its reusable binding defect is reproduced
and fixed in private v3 CPU controls, not retroactively rewritten or GPU-retested.
