## Materials And Limits

Twelve used embedded256px WebPs form Steel, Radial, Dial and Enamel color/normal/
roughness sets. Deterministic linear/radial brush fields, dark-dial grain and
enamel variation derive normal directions, normalized before lossless WebP packing.
Color and packed roughness/metal fields are lossy. Steel's anisotropy follows its
actual mesh UVs; no emission fabricates highlights. All source algorithms remain
in the builder; recipe images[] accurately declares no external source images.

These are artistic PBR approximations, not measured relief, certified alloy,
refraction or a calibrated compass. BLEND transparency is not physical optics.
Lossless packed normals do not imply zero discretization or filtering loss.
No downloaded compass mesh, branding, paid GPU or new dependency is used.

The model recovers23,732 bytes from the261,576-byte study. Public assets total
9,908,162 bytes:577,598 below the unchanged10MiB cap. The524,288-byte model target

## Observed REDs

Fourteen standalone cases first failed on the unrefined artifact with genuine
ERR_ASSERTION failures, not missing-module/ENOENT errors. The parent full run
observed129 existing passes/14 expected failures before any implementation.
Original geometry/procedural material declarations follow those failures.

The first candidate passed12 cases. Real faceted side triangles had collapsed UVs;
explicit two-triangle side UVs corrected that failure. The dial's closed manifold
also exposed a probe aimed exactly along a quantized vertex seam: the unshifted
ray missed while a1e-7 angular change hit the same surface. A64-ray nonaligned
sample replaces the seam-aligned probe, retaining exact one-hit/radius assertions,
a real circular positive and a square negative. Original diagnostics are retained.

Rendered review softened excessive concentric grain and adjusted only asset pose
and material factors. Independent review then found that case/lid skins filled
assembled hinge bores and the pin exceeded their radius. Two real REDs precede
moving the hinge outside both skins, offsetting the lid cup and exterior brackets,
adding a center case support and reducing the pin to .021 inside .024 bores.

Three further REDs precede local-feature/absolute-bound contact tolerance, actual
end-cap seating rather than terminal-quarter acceptance, and split lens-rim normals.
Buried cap seating uses parity only after closed/outward support validation;
open skins never gain containment acceptance. The lens coverage probe also exposed
an exact seam miss after root transforms changed; retained diagnostics confirm the
second surface at a1e-7 offset. Projected-triangle vertical sections now preserve
the same two-hit/.002-clearance checks at actual vertices and centroids, with a
1e-9 barycentric edge allowance plus edge-positive/outside-negative controls.

The current16-case suite passes actual geometry/material/
UV/budget checks, with adverse fixtures for filled apertures, open/inverted/mixed
shells, AABB-only contact, nested skins, midspan brackets and stale cap guards.
Actual GPU TRS expansion and inverse-transpose normals are checked, not inferred.
Independent exact-artifact reviews and final transport evidence are separate gates.
